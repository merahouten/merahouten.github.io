From Production Reliability to Data & AI Systems

I am an Incident Manager at Société Générale Assurances and an engineering student pursuing the Bachelor Informatique et Projets Numériques (IPN) at ISEP Paris. Working directly with enterprise production environments has given me a strong appreciation for how critical IT systems behave under pressure—where service continuity, rapid diagnostics, and structured coordination matter every day.

Today, I am channeling this foundation—production reliability + AI-accelerated software engineering—toward Data Engineering, Cloud Infrastructure, and Applied AI, while also bringing strong creative execution in photography, videography, video production, and community management.
